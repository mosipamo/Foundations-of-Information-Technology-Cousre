to make a new order: (Creating order)
	curl -X POST http://localhost:5000/orders -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"item\": \"item1\", \"quantity\": 2}"

to see all the orders: (Getting orders)
	curl http://localhost:5000/orders

to see the inventory of an item: (Checking inventory)
	curl http://localhost:5001/inventory/item3

to update inventory(just decrease an inventory, It does not create an order): 
	curl -X PUT http://localhost:5001/inventory/item1 -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"quantity\": 5}"
